Official Announcement

Finwin Edu Campus is proud to announce its official certification as a Private Medical Institution, approved by the Private Health Services Regulatory Council (PHSRC) of the Ministry of Health, under registration number PHSCH/OMI/626. This accreditation has been formally granted for the establishment and operation of our dedicated Nursing Training School.

This milestone reflects our unwavering commitment to academic excellence, rigorous quality assurance, and adherence to national healthcare standards. By securing this approval, Finwin Edu Campus is authorised to deliver professional nursing training programmes designed to cultivate highly skilled, compassionate, and competent healthcare professionals ready to meet modern medical demands.

We extend our sincere gratitude to the Ministry of Health and the PHSRC for their guidance throughout this rigorous evaluation process. This certification marks a monumental step forward in our mission to expand our healthcare education portfolio and shape the future of medical training.

We look forward to welcoming our inaugural cohort of nursing students and empowering the next generation of healthcare leaders.
