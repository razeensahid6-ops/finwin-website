About Finwin Campus

Expanding knowledge.
Inspiring innovation.

Finwin Edu Campus was registered on 23rd September 2019 and officially inaugurated on 4th February 2021 at No. 1094, Maradana Road, Borella, Colombo 08, Sri Lanka.

Our Beginning

Since its inception, the campus has remained dedicated to delivering uncompromised quality in education, ensuring that every student receives an academic experience of international standards.

Four pillars.
One quality framework.

Our guiding principle, "Quality Uncompromised," is reinforced by the four pillars of our quality framework.

01
Upholding accreditation standards

Keeping programmes aligned with recognised academic and professional requirements.

02
Ensuring timely delivery of the academic process

Supporting students through organised teaching, assessment, and academic schedules.

03
Fostering continuous improvement

Reviewing feedback and outcomes so learning quality keeps improving over time.

04
Building and sustaining stakeholders' trust

Maintaining transparent, dependable relationships with students, partners, and communities.

Our Values

Passion. Accountability. Diligence. Innovation. Fairness.

Passion

We work with energy and care, aiming to delight our students, partners, and community through quality education.

Accountability

We value reliability, personal responsibility, and the ability of staff and the organization to honor commitments.

Diligence

We stay productive, self-motivated, and committed to completing work efficiently while serving students well.

Innovation

We address development issues by looking beyond the obvious, experimenting thoughtfully, and stimulating creativity.

Fairness

We make decisions with moral care, equity, and respect for the consequences our actions have on others.
