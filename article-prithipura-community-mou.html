Finwin Edu Campus Partners with Prithipura Community

Finwin Edu Campus is proud to announce the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Prithipura Community, a well-respected institution dedicated to the care and welfare of individuals with special needs. The MoU was formally signed on 27 July 2026 by Ms. Pushpa Ramanayake, Director of Prithipura Community, and Mr. Rishard Fallil, Director of Compliance & AQ at Finwin Edu Campus.

This partnership marks an important milestone for our Caregiver and Nursing Assistant programmes, giving students the chance to complete a structured 192-hour internship within a genuine care environment.

Why This Partnership Matters

Classroom learning gives students the knowledge they need, but nothing prepares a future caregiver or nursing assistant quite like real, hands-on practice. Working with individuals with special needs calls for patience, empathy and a particular set of practical skills that can only be built through direct experience. This MoU ensures our students are not just taught the theory, but are given a genuine opportunity to put it into practice under proper guidance.

Through this collaboration, Finwin Edu Campus students will spend 192 hours at Prithipura Community, working alongside experienced staff and being directly involved in the care and support of individuals with special needs. This immersive experience is designed to build confidence, sharpen practical skills and deepen students' understanding of person-centred care.

What Students Will Gain

This internship offers far more than a line on a certificate. Students can expect to:

  • Develop practical caregiving skills, including personal care, mobility support and daily living assistance, under the supervision of trained professionals.
  • Build genuine empathy and communication skills through direct interaction with individuals with special needs and their families.
  • Understand real-world care settings, learning how a professional care community operates day to day.
  • Strengthen their employability, as hands-on experience with a recognised community partner is highly valued by future employers in healthcare and caregiving.
  • Grow personally, as many students describe this kind of experience as genuinely life-changing and formative for their work in care.

A Meaningful Step for Our Programmes

This MoU reflects Finwin Edu Campus's ongoing commitment to producing well-rounded, work-ready graduates. We believe that true competence in caregiving and nursing assistance is built at the intersection of solid theoretical knowledge and genuine practical exposure. Partnering with Prithipura Community allows us to offer exactly that.

We are equally grateful to Prithipura Community for welcoming our students into their environment and for their continued dedication to the wellbeing of individuals with special needs. This partnership is built on a shared belief: that compassionate, well-trained caregivers make a real difference in people's lives.

Looking Ahead

Finwin Edu Campus will continue to seek out partnerships that give our students meaningful, practical opportunities to grow both professionally and personally. The internship programme with Prithipura Community is one example of how we are working to bridge the gap between education and the real world of care.

We look forward to seeing our students take on this opportunity with dedication and heart, and we congratulate them in advance on the valuable experience they are about to gain.

For more information about the Caregiver and Nursing Assistant programmes at Finwin Edu Campus, or about this internship opportunity, please get in touch with our academic office.
